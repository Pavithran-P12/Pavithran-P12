My dream is simple: build more useful websites that help people solve real problems, without having to worry about money. If that sounds worth backing, you can help.

Support on Razorpay ↗

01The dream

I like understanding how things work, then making them work better. Some of my favourite builds started as someone else’s problem: a wedding studio that needed couples to find them, and a badminton organiser who needed to run 103 matches on five courts in one day.

I’d love to spend more of my time on work like that: websites and small platforms for people, communities and causes that need them but can’t always pay for them. Free of the pressure to make every project about money, so the focus stays on making things that actually help. A small part of making the world a better place.

02What your support does

  • Pays for domains, hosting and the tools that keep projects running.
  • Buys time to build for people and causes that can’t afford a developer.
  • Lets me say yes to ideas that matter more than they pay.

03How to support

Any amount helps. Choose what feels right on my Razorpay page and pay with UPI, card, net banking or a wallet. The payment is handled entirely by Razorpay.

Support on Razorpay ↗razorpay.me/@pavithranm

Support is a voluntary contribution towards my independent work. It isn’t a payment for a service, it’s non-refundable, and it isn’t tax-deductible.

04Other ways to help

Money isn’t the only way. Share this website with someone who needs a site or a tool built, tell me about a problem worth solving, or just say hello. You can reach me on WhatsApp or by email.
