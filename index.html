Curiosity is
the constant.

P.Developer.
Experimenter.
Always a work in progress.

I like understanding how things work.
Then figuring out how they could work better.

My way into software was through databases and backend development. Working close to the data taught me to value clarity, reliability, and the parts of an application you don't always see.

These days, I'm connecting that foundation to full-stack engineering: building with Angular and .NET, shaping useful interfaces, and experimenting with AI workflows and intelligent agents.

Outside the day job, I make small tools for everyday problems and the occasional playful web experiment. Sometimes the best way to learn something is to build it. And when I'm not building, I'm usually gaming. I share some of it on my NosGames YouTube channel.
